Broadcast audio · JavaScript and C#

Commercial Viewer

A Windows application I built with AI assistance that detects commercial breaks during sports broadcasts and controls when the receiver mutes and restores sound.

When should the sound come back?

A commercial can include footage that looks like live play. Commercial Viewer weighs broadcast cues before deciding that the game has returned. Its manual correction logic keeps evaluating new evidence after a viewer corrects a mistaken mute.

37 offline detector tests passed on Windows and macOS.

Kalshi Scout

A read-only ChatGPT plugin that brings sports data and market quotes into one research workflow.

What price can a researcher actually use?

The last trade can differ from the current asking price. Scout reads the ask, checks available size when reported and applies the requested price limit. Its local research journal keeps a record for later review.

Shopping research · Python

Lantern

A shopping assistant that checks whether two offers describe the same item before comparing prices.

When is a deal really a deal?

A blue desk lamp and a green one can share a product family. Lantern checks currency, condition and variant before accepting a barcode match or a brand and part-number match. That keeps a cheaper configuration from becoming the reference price.

blue → GTIN + condition + variant / green → Excluded

Event monitoring · Python

Ticket Watcher

A local watcher for comparing ticket listings against seat-count, price and timing constraints.

When should a ticket watcher look again?

As an event approaches, a fifteen-minute check can miss a short-lived listing. The watcher switches to five-minute checks when its configured window opens, then stops at the cutoff. The sample isolates this scheduling rule from marketplace access.

15-minute interval → 5-minute interval → stop
